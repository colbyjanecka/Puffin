# Puffin Keys - Mechanical Keyboard / MIDI Sequencer

*The Atlantic Puffin is not just any bird.*

It's a skilled flier, reaching speeds of 88km/h by flapping their wings up to 400 per minute.  They are also expert swimmers, diving up to 60 meters while using their wings to propel themselves through  water.

**Here, we have the Puffin of Mechanical Keyboards.**  

First, the Puffin Keys is an exceptional typer.  By utilizing low profile mechanical switches, it creates a fun and exception typing experience in a small portable package. 

Second, it serves as a powerful MIDI controller and sequencer that can communicate both wirelessly and the onboard TRS hardware jacks.

[**`Powered by Mania Synths`**](https://mania.fm/)

## Features

- Kailh Choc v1 Switches with Hotswap Sockets
- Per-Key RGB Backlighting with SK6812 LEDs
- 2 x TRS Type-A MIDI Jacks (In and Out)
- 3000 mAh LIPO Battery
- Bluetooth 5 and BLE 
- Dual 32bit Xtensa LX7 cores
- 0.91" OLED Display*
- Future Expansion Capabilities

**The screen can be replaced with alternative I2C input/output modules such as knobs, sensors, or CV ports.*
